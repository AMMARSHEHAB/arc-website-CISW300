Gaming Festival is a fictional student event concept celebrating console, PC and arcade games. Imagine a welcoming place to try a new game, meet teammates and enjoy friendly competition.

Better together

Start with a co-op round, join a small team challenge or cheer for a friend. Beginners and experienced players share the same goal: have a good time.

Read the play guide before joining a round.

Play with respect

Take turns, explain the controls to new players and keep the conversation friendly. Ask before changing another player’s settings, and pause for a break between sessions.
