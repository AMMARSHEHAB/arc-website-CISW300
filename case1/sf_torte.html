Homemade Pizza

Simple dough, fresh toppings

A crisp edge, a soft middle and bubbling mozzarella: this is my idea of a good homemade pizza. Start with a simple yeast dough, then finish with tomato sauce and your favorite light toppings.

Makes two 10-inch pizzas · Serves 4
Hands-on: about 25 minutes · Rise: 60–90 minutes

Get the ingredients · Start cooking

Before you begin

  • Measure everything
  • Use warm water
  • Allow rising time
  • Stretch gently
  • Keep toppings light
  • Heat the oven well

What you’ll need

  • 3 cups (375 g) all-purpose flour, plus a little for shaping
  • 1 cup (240 ml) warm water
  • 2 teaspoons instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ¾ cup crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 200 g mozzarella, shredded or torn into small pieces
  • A handful of fresh basil leaves
  • Optional: a small amount of sliced vegetables or chili flakes

Let’s make pizza

  1. Make the dough. Stir the flour, yeast, sugar and salt together. Add the water and oil. Mix into a dough, then knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth. If it is very sticky, dust with a little flour.
  2. Give it time. Put the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and leave in a warm spot for 60–90 minutes, until roughly doubled in size.
  3. Prepare the sauce. Stir the oregano into the crushed tomatoes. Keep the sauce thick enough to spread without making the base wet.
  4. Heat and shape. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C). Divide the dough in two. Stretch each piece into a 10-inch circle and place it on a lightly oiled baking sheet.
  5. Build your pizza. Spread a thin layer of sauce, leaving a small rim. Add mozzarella and a light scattering of optional toppings. Cook watery vegetables first and drain them well.
  6. Bake until golden. Bake for about 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden, the cheese bubbles and the underside is cooked. Ovens vary, so check the base before taking it out.
  7. Finish and enjoy. Add fresh basil after baking. Rest for 2 minutes, then slice. Repeat with the second pizza.

Kitchen notes

A crisp base

A thin layer of sauce helps the dough bake evenly. Give the oven time to reach its temperature before the pizza goes in.

Balance the toppings

More toppings are not always better. A small amount of vegetables lets the cheese melt and the dough cook through.

Let the dough relax

If the dough springs back as you shape it, cover it and rest for 10 minutes. It should become easier to stretch.
